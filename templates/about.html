Our story

A considered approach to modern investing.

AstroCapital Investment is a private investment company established in the United Kingdom in 2015. Our work spans cryptocurrency, foreign exchange, stocks, bonds, futures, and real estate.

We created AstroCapital to make professionally managed investment services easier to understand and access. Our team studies changing market conditions, develops financial strategies, and looks for opportunities across multiple asset classes.

We believe a strong investment relationship starts with clear communication. That means giving clients a simple way to view their account, manage funds, follow activity, and reach support when they need assistance.
